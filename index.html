    How to interpret the indicators

    Foreign direct investment. Inward and outward series are annual net flows, not stocks or announced projects. Negative inward flows remain negative and can reflect disinvestment or reverse flows.

    Current U.S. dollars. Dollar series use current prices and are not adjusted for inflation. Long-run nominal changes alone do not measure real growth.

    Data timing. Provider release dates vary. Snapshot retrieval time records when this copy was assembled, not the most recent observation year.
